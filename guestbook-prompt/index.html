John Doe's Obituary

John Doe of Dixon, Illinois, died peacefully at home on October 6, 2026, at the age of 66, with his family beside him.

Born in 1960, John spent thirty-one years as a machinist at the Rock River works, where he was known for arriving an hour early and for a tool chest nobody else was allowed to touch. He married Elaine in 1984. They raised three children, kept an unreasonable number of tomato plants, and spent most Augusts on the water at Lake Carroll.

He is survived by his wife Elaine; his children Marcus, Dana, and Ruth; and six grandchildren who knew him as the only adult who would take them fishing before sunrise.
