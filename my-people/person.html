Ralph's Obituaries

Excerpt from Chicago Sun-Times

Ralph Thomas passed away February 14, 2023, at the age of 86. Ralph was born in Fresno, California, on March 18, 1935, to his parents, Robert Thomas and Veronica Delgado. He is preceded in death by his brothers Raymond and Franklin, his son Anthony, and his beloved wife Patricia (Ramirez) Thomas. Ralph is survived by his daughters Elena and Sofia, his grandchildren, and a wide circle of friends.
